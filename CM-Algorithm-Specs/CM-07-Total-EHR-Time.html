Canonical Measure Algorithm Card

CM-07 — Total EHR Time

NB — Net Benefits EHR Efficiency Also known as: Total EHR Time  ·  Login Time  ·  Active EHR Time  ·  EHR Burden

Unit

Unit
Total EHR minutes per encounter
↓ Lower is better

A total EHR minute is any minute of logged activity in the EHR system by a given provider, across all task types — note creation, inbox management, order entry, results review, and any other EHR interaction. Minutes are summed per provider per encounter-equivalent period and normalized to completed encounters.

Gold Standard
Full EHR audit-log telemetry capturing all activity types across all EHR modules (documentation, inbox, orders, results, admin). Preferred — gives the broadest and most complete view of EHR burden.
Default / Practical
Documentation time + inbox time only, when full audit-log is unavailable. These two components are the most reliably extracted from most EHR telemetry systems and account for the largest share of total EHR time.
⚠ Ambiguities to Resolve
  • Scope of "EHR time." Different EHR telemetry fields vary in which activities they include. Before using any field, confirm whether it captures documentation only, documentation + inbox, or all EHR activity. Mixing narrow and broad definitions across pre/post windows invalidates the comparison. This measure is explicitly broader than CM-04 (Documentation Time).
  • Per-encounter vs. per-workday normalization. Some studies report total EHR time per day (Ma 2025: −19.95 min/day); others normalize to encounters. Per-encounter normalization is preferred when encounter volume itself may change after Suki adoption (see CM-22). Per-day is acceptable when encounter volume is stable.
  • Inbox and orders may dilute the Suki signal. Suki directly impacts documentation time; its effect on inbox and order entry is indirect and smaller. A large inbox component in the denominator will attenuate the observed effect size relative to a documentation-only measure. Report the component breakdown when possible.

Algorithm

1 Dashboard Metric — Cohort Average For the Suki admin console

Produces the average total EHR minutes per encounter per provider in the cohort for a given month. This is the broadest efficiency indicator in the CM suite: it captures all EHR time, not just documentation. Ma 2025 found a larger total-EHR-time reduction (−19.95 min/day) than documentation-time-alone reduction (−6.89 min/day) in the same cohort.

Units in this Algorithm
Primary
Total EHR minutes per encounter
The canonical unit for this measure — defined in the Unit card above. Averaged per provider per reporting month.
Secondary
Active providers in the cohort
The denominator. An active provider is one who has completed at least one Suki session in the reporting month and has passed their onboarding ramp period (default: 30 days post-adoption).
⚠ Providers still in the onboarding ramp should be excluded. EHR time may temporarily increase during initial Suki learning as providers adapt their workflow.
Cohort Average=∑ (avg total EHR min / encounter per provider, in the month)Number of active providers in the cohort
Reporting grain: one value per cohort per calendar month Pre / post boundary: each provider's Suki adoption date (first completed session) Exclude: providers still within their onboarding ramp period (default 30 days) Scope: all EHR activity types (documentation + inbox + orders + results review)
Pseudocode
For each calendar month M: active_providers = providers with ≥1 completed Suki session AND past ramp period For each provider p in active_providers: total_ehr_min[p] = SUM of all EHR activity minutes for provider p in month M (documentation + inbox + orders + results review) encounters[p] = completed encounters for provider p in month M ehr_per_enc[p] = total_ehr_min[p] / encounters[p] dashboard_value[M] = MEAN( ehr_per_enc[p] for p in active_providers )
Expected Trend — Total EHR Min / Encounter / Month

Cohort average should decrease after Suki adoption. The effect compounds as providers become proficient with ambient documentation. Ma 2025 reports −19.95 min/day total EHR time reduction — larger than the documentation-time-alone effect in the same study.

Suki Data — Scoped Role

Suki provides a partial proxy for the documentation component of total EHR time, but cannot observe inbox activity, order entry, or results review. Session duration from the Suki API reflects in-app documentation time only. The majority of total EHR time — inbox management, chart review, and orders — occurs entirely within the EHR and is invisible to Suki.

Suki's primary contributions to this measure are the adoption date (pre/post boundary) and the active usage flag (denominator definition). For the outcome signal itself, the EHR full audit-log is the authoritative source. Organizations that report only Suki session duration for this measure will systematically understate total EHR time and the true magnitude of any change.
